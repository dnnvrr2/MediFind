# Cagayan de Oro location coverage

The picker includes all 80 Cagayan de Oro barangays for immediate local filtering. The list is checked against the Philippine Statistics Authority city directory: https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/barangays/1030500000. A citywide entry and the existing Corrales Avenue sample entry are also available. Streets and landmarks are not a complete street-address directory; online search may add places only if their returned point is within the bundled city polygons.

Boundary geometry is extracted from James Faeldon's Philippines JSON Maps, the unsimplified 2011 CDO barangay GeoJSON: https://github.com/faeldon/philippines-json-maps/blob/master/2011/geojson/barangays/barangays-municity-988-cagayandeorocity.json. It is distributed under the MIT license, reproduced in CDO_BOUNDARIES_LICENSE.txt. Generated data rounds coordinates to six decimal places, normalizes the capitalization of Camaman-an, Canito-an and Pigsag-an, and derives representative interior map points. These are approximate historical boundaries, not current legal or cadastral determinations. Boundary-adjacent results should be confirmed manually; refresh the dataset before relying on it for real delivery coverage.

Point-in-polygon checks exclude outside-city Photon results, saved suggestions, restored location selections, and device locations. Holes and multiple polygons are supported. There is no reverse-geocoder request for detected coordinates: barangay lookup happens locally.

Use my location asks for foreground device/browser permission only after the user taps it. Denied access, unavailable location services, a 20-second positioning timeout, accuracy worse than 1.5 km, and outside-CDO positions produce clear messages. The detected barangay and reported accuracy are shown for confirmation. Only confirmation updates the browsing area. The picker shows a text confirmation without loading a map or sending the detected coordinates to OpenStreetMap. Maps are available separately on pharmacy, tracking, and rider delivery screens. No background tracking is enabled. Browser location needs HTTPS or localhost. Native builds must be rebuilt after adding the Expo Location config plugin.
